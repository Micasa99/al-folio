AICosmos

Educational multi-agent platform — incubated at Tsinghua, live at aicosmos.ai.

AICosmos turns AI from a tool into a teammate in the classroom — a multi-agent operating layer for education and research, incubated by Tsinghua University’s Department of Computer Science.

My role. Contributor — agent design and platform features. Status. Live at aicosmos.ai. Tagline. AI for Education × AI for Research — empowering teaching and discovery through agentic AI.

Why agents, not just chatbots?

Today’s AI in education is mostly a Q&A bot: it answers facts but doesn’t act with the learner. Two gaps remain:

  • Disembodied action — AI can’t touch the real workflow, so knowledge stays inert.
  • Decontextualization — knowledge is detached from social and physical context.

AICosmos closes both gaps by composing three classes of agents into a single platform:

Agent Role What it does
Tutor Agent Coach Guides learners through reasoning steps rather than handing answers.
Role-Play Agent Co-actor Plays a specific role to make interaction situated, not abstract.
Q&A Bot Reference Answers factual questions on demand.

Together they form an educational agent: an entity that can perceive a learning environment, plan autonomously, and act to support the teaching goal.

What’s underneath

  • Multi-agent orchestration for course-specific workflows
  • Tool use — agents can call into simulation, retrieval, and grading
  • Profile-driven — adapts to a learner’s history and curriculum
  • API-first — drops into existing teaching platforms
